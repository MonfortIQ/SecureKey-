About SecureKey Locksmith

Dedicated to safeguarding family homes and residential communities with honest upfront pricing, emergency rapid response, and master craftsmanship.

Our Journey

15+ Years of Dedicated Residential Security

Founded in 2010 by master locksmith Patrick Miller, SecureKey Locksmith began with a single mobile van and a simple commitment: provide honest, high-integrity locksmith services to local homeowners without the predatory pricing all too common in the emergency trades.

Over the past decade and a half, front door locks have evolved from simple pin-tumbler cylinders into biometric sensors, Bluetooth deadbolts, and Wi-Fi cloud-connected platforms. SecureKey has stayed ahead of every technological advancement. Today, our fleet of mobile workshops carries both classical lock-picking tools and cutting-edge computerized key decoders.
