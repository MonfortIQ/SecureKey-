Are Smart Locks Safe? Security Myths vs Facts

By Ethan Brooks October 02, 2025 2 min read

Smart lock security is a combination of choices.

Separate the hardware from the account

A smart lock still needs a sound physical installation. The door fit and strike matter alongside the app and access codes. Digital features vary between models, so compare the manufacturer’s documented functions for the precise product rather than assuming every smart lock offers the same controls.

Keep access management deliberate

Use a secure account password and any additional account protection the manufacturer offers. Decide who should be an administrator and who only needs guest access. Where individual codes are supported, use them thoughtfully and remove access that is no longer needed. Do not share an administrator account just to make a short visit easier.

Plan for ordinary interruptions

Batteries, phones, and networks can occasionally be unavailable. Ask which entry functions continue without internet and which require a hub or cloud service. Practice the specific model’s backup method while you are comfortably inside. Follow the manufacturer’s battery and firmware guidance; convenience is more useful when the household knows how the device behaves.

Your practical checklist

  • Check the exact model’s security and access features.
  • Review account owners and guest permissions.
  • Learn supported backup entry methods.
  • Follow official update and battery instructions.

For model-specific details, see the manufacturer’s documentation . This article is general template guidance; the exact hardware and installation need an individual assessment.
