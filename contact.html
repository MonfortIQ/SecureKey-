How quickly can a locksmith arrive?

Typical emergency arrival is 15–25 minutes. Your location, traffic, and technician availability can affect timing. Call dispatch for an estimate for your address.

Can I schedule a visit for another day?

Absolutely. Choose “Plan a visit” in the form and select your preferred day and time. Our team will confirm availability with you. Appointments run Monday through Saturday, 8 am–7 pm.

What information should I have ready?

Your service address, a phone number we can reach you on, and a brief description of the issue. If you know your lock's brand or model, include that too. Your technician may ask for proof that you are authorized to access the property.

Can you help with a smart lock upgrade?

Yes. Select Smart Lock Installation or Smart Lock Upgrade in the form, and tell us about your current door and lock. You can also explore our smart lock services .
