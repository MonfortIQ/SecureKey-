Residential Lock Rekeying Service

Keep your existing decorative door handles and deadbolts while rendering old keys completely useless. Perfect after moving, roommate changes, or lost keys.

Keep your hardware. Reset your key access.

Keep your existing decorative door handles and deadbolts while rendering old keys completely useless. Perfect after moving, roommate changes, or lost keys.

Lock rekeying is one of the smartest and most economical security decisions a homeowner can make. Rather than spending hundreds of dollars discarding high-quality handlesets, our certified locksmiths alter the internal tumblers inside your existing lock cylinders. Once re-pinned, previous keys cannot turn the lock, and you receive a fresh set of master-matched keys.

Common Situations Requiring This Service

  • Moving into a previously occupied house where prior owners or realtors kept spare keys.
  • Losing a house key without knowing who might have picked it up.
  • Ending a tenant lease, divorce, or roommate arrangement where key recovery is uncertain.
  • Carrying too many different keys for the front door, back door, garage entry, and patio.
  • Keys sticking or becoming hard to turn due to mismatched pin wear over time.

What's Included in Our Scope

Cylinder Deconstruction

Disassembling the lock cylinder to inspect tumbler springs and brass plug condition.

Precision Pin Replacement

Loading new bottom and master pins cut specifically to your new key depth configuration.

Single-Key Unification

Keying all exterior doors alike so one single key opens every entrance of your home.

New Laser Keys Included

Supplying fresh, precision-cut brass keys along with full lubrication and hardware tuning.

What this service makes easier

  • Retain suitable existing handles and deadbolts.
  • Replace old key access with a newly matched key.
  • Ask about one-key convenience for compatible cylinders.

A service built around your door

We inspect the cylinder and explain whether rekeying is suitable before recommending a replacement.

Our Step-by-Step Professional Process

  1. Home Security Survey
    We inspect every exterior door to identify lock brand profiles (Schlage, Kwikset, Yale).
  2. Cylinder Extraction
    We remove the internal lock cores cleanly without scratching exterior trim plates.
  3. Pin Configuration
    We calculate new pin heights and replace all internal wafers with hardened brass pins.
  4. Testing & Key Delivery
    We test your new keys on both sides of the door and provide multiple copies.
