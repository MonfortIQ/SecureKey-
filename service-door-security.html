Residential Door Security Upgrades

Over 85% of home break-ins occur via brute-force door kick-ins that splinter wooden door jambs. We fortify your entire entryway with heavy-gauge steel armor.

Look at the entrance as a complete system

Over 85% of home break-ins occur via brute-force door kick-ins that splinter wooden door jambs. We fortify your entire entryway with heavy-gauge steel armor.

Even the strongest deadbolt in the world is useless if the wooden door jamb holding the strike plate is made of soft pine secured by flimsy 3/4-inch screws. A single kick easily splits standard door frames. SecureKey Locksmith transforms residential doors into fortified entryways using steel strike boxes, 3-inch anchor screws driven deep into the home’s framing studs, and reinforced hinge pins.

Common Situations Requiring This Service

  • Wooden door jamb has hairline cracks or previous split damage from settling or force.
  • Strike plate is attached with short 3/4-inch screws anchored only into soft decorative trim.
  • Door frame flexes noticeably when pushed, indicating a weak or hollow cavity.
  • Homeowner lives in an urban or secluded neighborhood desiring maximum peace of mind.
  • Thin hollow-core or weak wooden doors requiring wrap-around steel edge reinforcement.

What's Included in Our Scope

Heavy-Gauge Steel Strike Plate

Installing an extended 11-gauge steel reinforcement plate with multi-point anchors.

3-Inch Stud Anchor Screws

Driving hardened steel lag screws through the jamb directly into the 2x4 wall studs.

Door Edge Wrap Plate

Reinforcing the area around the deadbolt cylinder against prying or splitting.

Security Hinge Screws

Upgrading hinge hardware so the door cannot be pried or kicked off its pivot.

What this service makes easier

  • Review the lock, strike, hinges, and frame together.
  • Prioritize improvements to suit the door and budget.
  • Retain comfortable everyday operation.

A service built around your door

The consultation considers hardware and the condition of the entrance, with a scope agreed before changes are made.

Our Step-by-Step Professional Process

  1. Security Weakness Audit
    We inspect door material, jamb gap tolerance, and hinge integrity.
  2. Deep Cavity Mortising
    We deepen the bolt cavity to allow full 1-inch deadbolt throw penetration.
  3. Stud Anchoring
    We drive 3-inch hardened screws through the strike plate into solid wall timber.
  4. Impact Testing
    We verify that the door shuts tight with zero rattle and maximum physical rigidity.
