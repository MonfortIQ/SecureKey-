Residential Locksmith & Smart Lock Upgrade Services

From 24/7 urgent lockout assistance to cutting-edge biometric smart deadbolt installations, we deliver precision craftsmanship and total home protection.

Emergency Home Lockout

Locked out of your home? Our mobile locksmiths arrive in 15–25 minutes with non-destructive bypass tools to restore access safely without damaging your door frame.

Residential Rekeying

Change your cylinder pins so previous keys immediately stop working without the cost of full hardware replacement. Perfect for moves or lost keys.

Lock Repair

Fix jammed cylinders, loose handles, sticking bolts, and worn internal springs. We diagnose mechanical friction and restore effortless latch operation.

Service Details

Lock Replacement

Replace outdated or compromised entry door locks with durable modern locksets. Choose from premium brass finishes, lever sets, and high-security hardware.

Service Details

Deadbolt Installation

Enhance your front and back doors with ANSI Grade 1 heavy-duty deadbolts. Precision borehole cutting and reinforced steel strike plate installation.

Service Details

Smart Lock Installation

Professional setup of modern keypad and Wi-Fi smart locks (Yale, Schlage, August). Includes bridge configuration, mobile app pairing, and family training.

Service Details

Smart Lock Upgrade

Upgrade older basic electronic keypads to modern Matter, biometric fingerprint, Apple Home Key, and Wi-Fi remote-controlled smart deadbolt systems.

Service Details

Key Duplication

Broken Key Extraction
