Traditional Hardware vs. Smart Security

See why thousands of homeowners are switching to smart lock systems for daily convenience and enhanced residential peace of mind.

Security Feature Traditional Mechanical Lock Smart Digital Security Lock
Physical Keys Required. Risk of loss, copying, or leaving inside. Optional. Enter via fingerprint, PIN, or smartphone.
Remote Access & Checking Impossible. Must return home to check locked status. Check and lock/unlock anywhere in the world.
Guest / Cleaner Access Must hide physical key under mat or meet in person. Send temporary or recurring timed entry PINs.
Auto-Lock Feature None. Door remains unlocked if forgotten. Locks automatically 30-180 seconds after closing.
Activity Log & Alerts No record of who unlocked or entered the home. Real-time smartphone alerts with exact timestamp.
Tamper & Forced Entry Alarms None. Only physical bolt resistance. Built-in siren triggers on multiple failed attempts.
Smart Home Automation No integration with lights, thermostats, or security. Triggers lighting, disarms security cameras on entry.

Our Smart Lock Installation Process

We don't just screw a lock into your door. We ensure flawless mechanical latch alignment, Wi-Fi bridge stability, and in-person homeowner mobile training.

1

Security Consultation

We inspect your door material, jamb thickness, borehole alignment, and Wi-Fi signal strength.

2

Hardware Selection

We recommend the ideal lock model (Yale, Schlage, August, Level) to match your door aesthetics.

3

Precision Fitting

We adjust the strike plate and mortise cavity so the motorized deadbolt glides without friction.

4

App & Bridge Setup

We connect the Wi-Fi bridge, pair the iOS/Android apps, configure fingerprint scans, and set guest PINs.

5

Final Security Test

We test auto-lock thresholds, test voice control routines, and provide full hands-on family demonstration.
