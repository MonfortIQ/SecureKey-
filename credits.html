About this template

A public-facing residential locksmith and smart-lock website built with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript. No admin dashboard or backend authentication is included.

Photography and illustrative content

The local collection combines AI-generated demo imagery and two licensed stock photographs. People, products, and locations are illustrative; they are not actual staff, customers, or branded product endorsements. Business statistics, sample testimonials, addresses, prices, and credentials must be replaced with verified client information.

See the asset manifest for image dimensions and subject mapping. Source images are optimized into WebP with smaller card variants where appropriate. Native dimensions are documented; images are not represented as 4K assets unless their actual dimensions qualify.
