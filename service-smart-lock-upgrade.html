Smart Lock Upgrade & System Modernization

Replace obsolete push-button keypads or first-generation electronic locks with lightning-fast biometric sensors, Matter protocols, and tamper alerts.

Build on what already works

Replace obsolete push-button keypads or first-generation electronic locks with lightning-fast biometric sensors, Matter protocols, and tamper alerts.

Technology moves rapidly. Older electronic locks from 5 to 10 years ago lack remote Wi-Fi access, wear down keypad numbers visibly (exposing your PIN), and consume batteries rapidly. SecureKey Locksmith provides seamless smart lock upgrades, retrofitting the newest biometric and Matter-enabled smart deadbolts into your existing doors.

Common Situations Requiring This Service

  • Older keypad numbers are faded or worn, allowing onlookers to deduce your code.
  • First-generation electronic locks lack mobile app access, requiring manual code programming.
  • Frequent disconnection from home automation systems or obsolete Zigbee/Z-Wave hubs.
  • Desire to upgrade to biometric fingerprint touch or Apple Home Key tap-to-unlock.
  • Need for automated activity history logs and real-time remote door status alerts.

What's Included in Our Scope

Obsolete Hardware Decommissioning

Clean removal of old keypads and patching of any exposed paint lines or holes.

Next-Gen Smart Lock Install

Mounting newest generation hardware (Yale Assure 2, Schlage Encode Plus, Ultraloq).

Matter & Thread Integration

Connecting to modern mesh protocols for instant response and ultra-low battery drain.

Biometric Enrollment

Scanning family fingerprints and configuring dual-factor authentication.

What this service makes easier

  • Review whether a retrofit or full replacement is suitable.
  • Check your phone, network, and existing smart home setup.
  • Test the supported features after the upgrade.

A service built around your door

An upgrade starts with compatibility, rather than assuming every existing lock can accept every device.

Our Step-by-Step Professional Process

  1. Existing Hardware Audit
    We examine your current electronic lock setup and identify ideal replacement models.
  2. Removal & Door Preparation
    We remove old units and polish door surfaces to ensure seamless coverage.
  3. Next-Gen Lock Installation
    We install the new smart deadbolt with weather-resistant rubber gasket seals.
  4. Network Commissioning
    We pair with your smart home ecosystem and migrate your favorite user access codes.
