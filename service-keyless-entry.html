Keyless Entry Installation Services

Leave heavy keyrings behind. Keyless entry systems provide backlit illuminated touchscreens, custom access PINs, tamper alarms, and weather-sealed housings.

Give each household member a suitable way in

Leave heavy keyrings behind. Keyless entry systems provide backlit illuminated touchscreens, custom access PINs, tamper alarms, and weather-sealed housings.

Keyless entry systems provide the ideal blend of high security and everyday convenience for busy families, fitness enthusiasts, and rental property managers. No more hiding physical keys under front porch flower pots or panicking when children forget their house keys. Unlock your door with a personalized 4- to 8-digit PIN code.

Common Situations Requiring This Service

  • Children frequently lose physical house keys coming home from school.
  • Frustration with carrying keys during neighborhood runs, dog walks, or bike rides.
  • Need to provide easy, secure access for housekeepers, pet sitters, or contractors.
  • Worry that hidden spare keys outside might be discovered by unauthorized persons.
  • Desire for backlit illuminated keypads for easy entry in dark porches at night.

What's Included in Our Scope

Electronic Deadbolt Install

Mounting heavy-duty touchscreen or push-button electronic deadbolt.

Weatherproof Gasket Fit

Installing exterior neoprene seals to prevent rain and moisture ingress.

Code Programming

Configuring master codes, user PINs, and single-use temporary guest access codes.

Auto-Relock Activation

Setting custom 30-to-180 second automatic deadbolt relocking timers.

What this service makes easier

  • Compare supported PIN, app, and other entry options.
  • Set up guest access on models that support it.
  • Review backup access and account ownership.

A service built around your door

Setup is tailored to the chosen device. We explain which functions need an account, bridge, or internet connection.

Our Step-by-Step Professional Process

  1. Door Preparation
    We verify standard 2-1/8 inch borehole and strike cavity depth.
  2. Hardware Mounting
    We install the exterior touch module and secure the interior motorized drive.
  3. Battery & Gasket Setup
    We install long-life industrial alkaline batteries and moisture seals.
  4. PIN Code Programming
    We program your family access codes and teach you how to modify PINs anytime.
